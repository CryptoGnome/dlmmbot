The fire-escape checklist.

Six exit priorities, in strict order. If P0 says run, nothing below it gets a vote.

P0

Safety

Pool dead, price crash, TVL drain, RugCheck flip, or a watched holder dumping — close immediately at high slippage, blacklist the token.

close now
P1

Stop loss

Position value down 25% against entry — cut it, cooldown the token. Capital preservation outranks the thesis.

cut
P2

Rotation

Fees decayed for consecutive polls, or max age reached — the position is dead weight; recycle capital to the queue.

recycle
P3

Above range

Price traveled up through the range and stayed there — take profit, bank a slice, optionally arm follow mode for the next dip.

take profit
P4

Claim

Unclaimed fees crossed the threshold — claim and convert to SOL. Fees are the product; they get banked, not re-risked.

bank fees
P5

Below range

Price fell under the ladder and stayed past the grace window — cut it. A wick gets tolerance; a trend gets an exit.

cut
Capital preservation first

Six brakes between the market and your wallet.

Every sizing decision comes from your own measured ledger — and several systems exist only to say no.

Sizing

Half-Kelly, from your ledger

Position size estimated from your own closed trades, halved for estimation error, hard-capped per position. Reads negative edge? Entries stop.

Brake

Circuit breaker

Measured daily loss beyond your set percent of the wallet — no new entries for 24 hours. Losses are measured from the wallet, not the chart.

Brake

Cluster brake

Several hard stops inside one window means the market is the problem, not the picks — entries pause for a cool-down.

Filter

Regime filter

SOL itself dumping shrinks every new position — or pauses entries entirely. The bot doesn't buy dips in a falling market at full size.

Gate

Paper → live promotion

Live isn't suggested until paper mode strings together seven consecutive profitable days — measured the same way live PnL is.

Lock

Double-locked live mode

Real funds move only when the config file and the environment both say live. One flag is never enough. Paper needs no wallet at all.
